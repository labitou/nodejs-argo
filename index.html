Why Oceans Matter

Oceans cover more than 70% of Earth's surface and support millions of species. They regulate the climate, provide food, produce oxygen, and connect people around the world.

Protecting marine ecosystems is essential for maintaining biodiversity and ensuring a sustainable future.

Major Threats to the Ocean

Plastic Pollution

Millions of tons of plastic waste enter the ocean every year, harming marine animals and damaging ecosystems.

Climate Change

Rising temperatures and ocean acidification threaten coral reefs and marine life.

Overfishing

Unsustainable fishing practices reduce marine populations and disturb the balance of ocean ecosystems.

What We Can Do

  • Reduce the use of single-use plastics.
  • Recycle and dispose of waste responsibly.
  • Support sustainable seafood choices.
  • Participate in beach and ocean clean-up activities.
  • Spread awareness about marine conservation.

A Better Future for Our Oceans

Every action matters. Governments, organizations, communities, and individuals can work together to restore ocean health.

By protecting the ocean today, we protect life on Earth tomorrow.
